# Book club notes style

Notes for this family of groups follow this format (details layered on top of the lens structure):

- `## Overview`: **must include a `- Book: Title (Author)` line** · attendee names · ratings (per person → average; transcribed numbers can be misheard — flag when unclear) · one-line flow
- `## Attendance and talk share`: table. Attendees whose voice wasn't separated get an honest "not separated"
- `## What was discussed`: numbered `### 1.` sections. **Organize the book discussion per participant** — under a "Name (rating · one-line take)" heading, group that person's impressions, interpretations, and direct quotes. Don't scatter them into topic bullets
- `### Where opinions diverged`: interpretive disagreements get their own section
- `## Memorable lines` / `## Insights` / `## Next time` (next book, announcements)
- Check surrounding context before using any quote. **A name outside the pack.md roster appearing in the notes signals a speaker mismapping** — cross-check the roster, and never quote an unconfirmed speaker
